# Glossary

A plain-English glossary of records-management, legacy-data, and migration terminology.

Written for the records manager rather than the developer. Some terms are technical, most are not.

## What this is for

Public sector organisations work with vocabulary that spans records management, IT operations, procurement, and audit. The terms mean different things to each audience. This glossary tries to give one plain-English definition per term that everyone in the room can share.

## What it isn't

An exhaustive index of every industry term. If it's not here, it's either specialised enough to warrant its own document, or we haven't yet needed it in our own work.
