# Contributing

Suggestions for new terms, better definitions, and small corrections are welcome.

## How to suggest a change

The lightest-weight way is to [open an issue](https://github.com/archivahq/glossary/issues/new) describing the term or edit you'd like to see, and why. We read every one.

If you'd rather propose the exact wording yourself, open a pull request against `glossary.md` with your suggested addition or edit.

## What we're looking for

- **Plain English.** The audience is a records manager, not a developer. Technical terms are welcome, but they need to be explained in ordinary language.
- **Precision over comprehensiveness.** A short, exact definition is more useful than a long, hedged one.
- **Vendor-neutral.** The glossary describes categories of thing (databases, systems, formats), not specific products. Named examples are fine where they illustrate the category.
- **One definition per term.** If a term genuinely means different things in different contexts, we split it into separate entries rather than list caveats.

## What we're not looking for

- Marketing language, comparative claims, or promotional content of any kind
- Definitions that require the reader to already know another specialist vocabulary
- Terms so specialised they only make sense inside one organisation
